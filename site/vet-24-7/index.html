Official lines

24/7 poison control & emergency lines

These are the two official animal poison-control centers commonly used in the United States and Canada. Call one of them if you think a pet has swallowed something toxic, and go to an emergency clinic if the pet is already in distress.

ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center

24/7, 365 days a year · United States & Canada

Call (888) 426-4435

If you think a pet may have ingested a potentially poisonous substance, this is the ASPCA’s official 24-hour poison-control line. A consultation fee may apply — confirm the current fee when you call.

Official ASPCA Poison Control page

Pet Poison Helpline

24/7 · United States & Canada

Call (855) 764-7661

A 24/7 animal poison-control service for pet owners and veterinarians in the United States and Canada. A per-incident fee applies (their site currently lists $99 USD, with follow-up consultations included — confirm the current amount when you call or chat).

A short guide

When to go, and when to call

Go immediately

  • Trouble breathing, or gums that look blue or very pale
  • Unproductive retching, or a swollen, hard belly (possible bloat)
  • Heavy bleeding or major trauma
  • A seizure, or sudden collapse
  • Suspected toxic ingestion — including chocolate, xylitol, grapes, lilies, human medication or antifreeze
  • Heatstroke or hypothermia

Call an after-hours vet tonight

  • Repeated vomiting or diarrhea
  • Sudden limping or severe pain
  • An eye injury
  • Inability to urinate
  • Persistent lethargy — a pet that will not settle or will not get up

Pawpartout™ is an information tool, not veterinary advice. When in doubt, call or go to an emergency clinic immediately.
