Affiliate disclosure

How Pawpartout earns from the links on its shop pages.

Last updated November 27, 2026.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Which links

The “Check price on Amazon” buttons in Paw Shop and Gifts for pets, and the Amazon buttons on the Books page, are Amazon affiliate links. If you buy through one of them, we may earn a commission. You pay the same price.

Affiliate links are marked for search engines with rel="sponsored". Links to makers’ official pages are not affiliate links.

What it does not change

A commission never changes what we pick or what a card says. We do not show Amazon prices or photos: each button opens the current Amazon listing, where the price and availability are up to date.

We have not tested these products ourselves. Product details come from each maker’s official page. Pictures on the cards are illustrations of the product type, not photos of the exact item.

No ads

Pawpartout shows no advertising. Affiliate links are the only way the shop pages earn money.
