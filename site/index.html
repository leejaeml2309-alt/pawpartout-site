The story

In 2016, Marc moved from France to Korea with his two cats, Lili and Lilou. He was not prepared, and the flight turned into a nightmare. Travelling with two cats meant they could not even sit together. The space under the seat was tiny and the carrier far too small. Frightened by the noise of the plane, Lilou hurt her paw trying to get out, and Marc had to insist before the crew let him take her to the restroom to calm her down.

That flight is where PetPolicyCheck began: the clear, checked information he wished he had had before take-off.

Animals have always been at the heart of Marc's life. Lili and Lilou are still by his side, along with Future, his shih tzu. He has also lost two rescue dogs, and he has supported animal rescue with donations and volunteer pet transport, in France and in Korea.

Pawpartout™ brings all of his projects together in one place. Like a passe-partout, the key that opens every door, it is meant to open the way for people who move with their animals: fewer surprises, better preparation, and journeys that respect those who never chose to travel.
